# Some students can always go

Some students have a medical need, often written into an IEP or 504 plan, that
means they must be able to leave whenever they need to. An Exempt Student
already never ran out of their Pass Allowance, but they could still be held
back by a Pass Limit, made to wait in the Line, or stopped by No-Pass Time,
and teachers had to step in with their PIN each time. Now a teacher can mark a
student **Can always go**: nothing at the kiosk ever holds them back, and they
go straight out, never into a Line. Every trip is still recorded, and marked
in History so the teacher can see it. We chose one checkbox that lifts every
rule over separate switches for each rule because a teacher thinks "this
child must always be able to go", and a medical need doesn't wait for the
Restroom to be free.

## Consequences

- An Always-Go Student's trip takes none of any Pass Limit's spots, as if they
  had gone somewhere that doesn't count toward it. A medical trip never keeps
  another student waiting, and a full Restroom may briefly hold one more than
  its limit.
- Every pass they take is marked, whether or not a rule would have stopped
  them that time. The mark is frozen when they leave, like the pass's other
  marks, so unticking the box later doesn't change past trips — and it is
  what tells the kiosk the trip takes no spot.
- Students who were already exempt stay exempt from the Pass Allowance only.
  Nobody is given more than the teacher chose. Both choices stay on the
  student's edit dialog; Can always go includes the allowance exemption.
- As with the Pass Allowance exemption, the kiosk never shows that a student
  can always go; the destination may still say Full while they leave.
- A kiosk on the version before this, until it refreshes, is sent such a
  student as exempt, so it lifts their Pass Allowance and holds them back for
  everything else, as before.
